Industrial Tier Classification Criteria

Algorithmic rules for Gold, Silver, and Bronze tier assignment

⭐
GOLD TIER (Flagship OEMs & Major Tier-1 Plants)
Rule: Google Rating ≥ 4.5 AND Review Count ≥ 100
Established market leaders, automotive OEMs, heavy engineering corporations, and ISO-certified manufacturing hubs with highest market reputation and public trust (e.g. Tata Motors, Bharat Forge, SKF India, Sandvik Asia, Forbes Marshall).
🛡️
SILVER TIER (Qualified MSMEs & Precision Tooling Units)
Rule: Google Rating ≥ 4.0 AND Review Count ≥ 25
Reliable, verified industrial vendors, component fabricators, CNC machine shops, and certified MSME engineering units with strong operational feedback (e.g. Thermax Boilers, Kalyani Technoforge, Endurance Technologies, KSB Pumps).
🏭
BRONZE TIER (Emerging Workshops & Specialized Fabricators)
Rule: Rating < 4.0 OR Reviews < 25 (or unrated)
Emerging job shops, niche fabrication workshops, and micro industrial suppliers that are physically located in the industrial zone but possess a nascent or developing online review presence.
💡 Manager Override: You can click any company row to open the Edit Details Drawer and manually override its Tier category based on your direct business engagement or site inspection.
